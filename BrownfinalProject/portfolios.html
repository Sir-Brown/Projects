The Importance of Electronic Portfolios

What is an electronic portfolio?

Paraphrasing, an electronic portfolio, or ePortfolio, is a digital collection of someone's work, achievements, and other information that demonstrates their learning journey. According to the University of waterloo, academic ePortfolios can also capture other aspects of a person’s life, such as volunteer experiences, employment history, extracurricular activities, and more. "In other words, ePortfolios document and make visible student learning. But a good ePortfolio should be more than just a collection of products."

Importance of an electronic portfolio?

Portfolios are a great way to demonstrate the competencies and accomplishments you would list on a resume or talk about in an interview. ePortfolios help you showcase your proven work to potential employers. It presents evidence of your relevant skills and abilities. Additionally, archiving samples of your work to a portfolio is a great way to keep track of your accomplishments and document when you acquired key competencies. Having everything you’ve done in one place can also be useful to reference in preparation for annual performance reviews, professional development conversations, networking or if you’re pursuing future professional opportunities.

My Job Career & How an ePortfolio helps

The career I am interested in pursuing is along the lines of Computer Science, specifically software development. In conclusion, an ePortfolio would allow me to showcase my work, create a memorable first impression, define my strengths and weeknesses, establish credability, and give employers a glimpse of what to expect when working with me.

What should an ePortfolio include?

An ePortfolio should include:

  • Work Samples
  • Biography/About me
  • Credentials
  • Resume
  • Refrences/Testimonies
  • Services
  • Contact Information
  • Organization (if applicable)
