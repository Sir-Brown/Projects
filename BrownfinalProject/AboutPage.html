About Me

D’Jaine Brown is the name, and creativity is the game . It was not always like this though, or at least that is what I would say if that were true ; however, it is not. Ever since I can remember, I’ve aspired to create and design things. It started off as creating sketches that would turn into paintings, and then developed into “sculptures” made of cardboard, tape, glue, and paper. Not long after that, I turned to digital creation and that opened up a whole new can of worms for me.

As aforementioned, getting into digital art introduced me to a plethora of new opportunities to express my creativity. The main options I went with, though, were Blender, Unreal Engine, FL Studio, Edjing, Adobe Photoshop, Wix, Canva, and a few more. This resulted in me developing an affinity for graphic designing and web authoring. It wasn’t until high school that I got into coding. That is when I started taking classes in HTML and SQL, and did courses in Javascript and CSS.

During that period, I learned the basics, but it wasn’t until college that I started to progress further in most of the areas listed earlier. For example, I started doing graphic designing a bit more professionally and started venturing into photography. Currently, I am enrolled in college and seeking an education in computer science focusing on software development. I am increasing my experience with HTML, CSS, Javascript, C++, Python, Java, and C#. I expect to leave more knowledgeable than when I started and I plan to use the skills I have and will acquire during my career.

Now with all the formalities out the way, allow me to move on to more personal information. After all, this is an about me page and the professional side isn't all there is. I enjoy cool lemonade and come equipped with a good sense of humor, alongside a strong work ethic of course. I like films in the sci-fi, horror, and comedy categories and have a similar preference for TV shows with the inclusion of dramas. When it comes to music, I’m an “I listen to whatever” type of person, so it depends on the day and how I feel. As for food, I’m not particularly picky. Also, I identify as “A man of culture” as I love the meme culture and I believe the right meme can uplift anyone's mood.
