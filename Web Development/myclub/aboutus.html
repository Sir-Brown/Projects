Welcome to our Comic Club!

Make yourself at home!

As stated on our home page, we're not like any ole' book club. Our Comic Club is a social group where members gather to share their love for comics, graphic novels, and manga.

Here’s a brief rundown of what we're about:

  1. Purpose:
    • To bring together comic enthusiasts for discussions, sharing recommendations, and exploring different genres and styles.
    • To foster a community where members can express their passion for comics and develop a deeper appreciation for the medium.
  2. Activities:
    • Reading Sessions: Members read and discuss selected comics, similar to a book club format.
    • Artist Workshops: Sessions that teach drawing, inking, and storytelling techniques used in comics.
    • Guest Speakers: Where possible, inviting comic artists, writers, or industry professionals to share insights and experiences.
    • Creative Challenges: Encouraging members to create their own short comics or collaborative stories.
    • Comic Swaps and Recommendations: Sharing and swapping favorite comics or new finds among members.
  3. Meetings:
    • Typically held weekly, every Friday at 5:30 P.M.
    • Meetings can be in person or online via platforms like Discord or Zoom. Announcements will be made via our website and Discord.
  4. Membership:
    • Free to Join.
    • Open to anyone interested in comics, from beginners to seasoned readers.
  5. Benefits:
    • A sense of community and shared interest.
    • Exposure to new comics, styles, and genres.
    • Opportunities to improve comic-related skills, such as drawing or storytelling.

Overall, this club is a fun and engaging way to connect with others who share a love for comics, providing both a social outlet and a creative space.
