What distinguishes them?

The clients that are looking for web design and development want functional, visually appealing and custom websites that are easy to manage and navigate through. As this is a small business, we would mainly appeal to startups as they would not be looking for large scale websites with over 1000 pages and continuous maintenance.These clients may overlap with the rest as they may wish to include various graphics throughout their website alongside additional coding for the back end, but they would mainly come to us for web development services.

The clients that are looking for graphic designing and printing want high quality, visually striking, and brand aligned designs that leave an impression. Furthermore, they may require print ready formats, alongside digital samples. These clients may overlap with the ones who want websites, or coding services, but the main difference is that these clients only want designs. They do not wish for an entire website nor do they need code for any software or applications.

The clients that are looking for coding want high performance and reliable code with a strong focus on security, and efficiency. These clients are more likely to have a technical background but lack the specialized coding expertise and that is where we come in.

Design Roles and Responsibilities

Project manager

Responsibilities:

  • Ensure deadlines and project milestones are met
  • Communicate with clients/stakeholders to understand their needs and expectations.
  • Maintain quality control across all services, resolving disputes and maintaining morale.
  • Oversee and assign tasks to team members based on expertise.

Strategist

Responsibilities:

  • Create the Web site development strategy
  • Brainstorm the website's mission statement.
  • Determines what our intended site users are looking for.
  • Sets project goals.

Content specialist

Responsibilities:

  • Research.
  • Analysis.
  • Organization.
  • Writing and editing.
  • Choosing the most appropriate text, images and multimedia to achieve the site's goals.

Usability expert

Responsibilities:

  • Test the Web site throughout development with intended users in mind.
  • Determine the site's usability compared to similar sites.
  • Ensure the site is culturally appropriate and follows accessability guidelines.

Designer (Web and Graphic)

Responsibilities:

  • Create visually appealing and user-friendly website layouts.
  • Design wireframes and mockups using tools like Figma, Adobe XD, or Sketch.
  • Create graphic images and/or branding materials (logos, business cards, brochures, posters, etc.).
  • Create digital assets for websites and social media.
  • Work closely with developers to ensure design consistency.

Developer (Web and Software)

Responsibilities:

  • Convert web designs into functional websites using HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and frameworks like React, Vue.js, or WordPress.
  • Programming the front-end UI and back-end application.
  • Develop custom software, automation scripts, and AI tools.
  • Maintain and troubleshoot website functionality.
  • Ensure website responsiveness and cross-browser compatibility.
  • Optimize performance and scalability of software projects.
  • Testing, revising and re-testing multiple times.

Marketer

Responsibilities:

  • Locate potential customers.
  • Reach out to them.
  • Bring them to the site.
  • Convert them into leads and sales.

How responsibilities are assigned

To ensure all services are covered efficiently:

Project Intake & Assignment:

  • The Project Manager assigns projects based on expertise (e.g., a branding project goes to the Graphic Designer, a website project to the Web Designer & Developer).

Collaboration across roles:

  • The Project Manager ensures that all member communicate efficiently.
  • The Project Manager ensures clients receive updates and final products meet expectations.

Product Creation:

  • Web Designers & Developers work together on websites.
  • Graphic Designers support Web Designers with digital assets.
  • Software Developers handle technical features and integrations.
