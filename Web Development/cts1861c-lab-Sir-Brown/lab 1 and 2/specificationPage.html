Design

How does it look and feel?

Page Layout

  • Clean and modern design with a structured grid layout.
  • Whitespace usage to improve readability and aesthetics.
  • Sticky navigation bar for easy site access.
  • Adheres to web UI design principles.
  • Adheres to visual design principles.

Typography

  • Headings: Bold, modern sans-serif (or any other easily readable font).
  • Body text: Readable, clean sans-serif (must thematically match headings even if different font-family is used).

Color Scheme

  • Primary Color: Deep blue to signify trust and professionalism (Example Color, not final).
  • Accent Color: Lighter blue to signify innovation and creativity (Example Color, not final).
  • Background: Light gray/white for a clean look (Example Color, not final).

Images and Logos

  • High resolution images showcasing past work and client collaborations.
  • Custom vector illustrations to highlight services visually as well as social media vectors in footer.
  • High resolution client logos available for preview.

Interactive Page Widgets

  • Testimonials section at the bottom of the homepage.
  • Hover effects on images, buttons, and links.
  • Dark mode toggle button for user preference.
  • Contact form on contact page.
  • Hamburger menu for mobile users.
