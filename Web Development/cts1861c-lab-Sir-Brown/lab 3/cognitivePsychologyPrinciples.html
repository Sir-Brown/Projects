Cognitive Psychology Principles

Problem Scenario:

  1. Users dislike having to think and work hard to get tasks done.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      This means our site should be familiar. Users should be able to navigate easily, find information quickly, and complete actions with minimal effort, almost as if they've been here before. A confusing layout, completely new UI, or excessive steps will frustrate users causing them to leave.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      It is highly relevant as our audience are looking for web and design services, and expecting smooth searching while doing so. Breaking the ordinary flow and stop their momentum might lead to them leaving.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Clear navigation bar with well-labeled menu options.
      • Minimalist design to avoid overstimulation.
      • Concise service descriptions.
      • Responsiveness in the event the users are view from a mobile device.
  2. Users have limitations.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      Users can't handle too much information at once. This means that giving them too many choices, complex layouts, or excessive text can lead to cognitive overload.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      It is very relevant as overloading users with information may prompt them leave the site without taking action.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Limit choices.
      • Use bit-sized content.
      • Utilize whitespace, visual hierarchy, and color weight to guide their eyes.
  3. Users make mistakes.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      Forms, navigation, and interactions should be error-tolerant to prevent user frustration (example: not being told where you went wrong in a form will cause an issue).

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Very relevant. Users will be completing forms on and navigating through our website, They may make mistakes, and the site should help them recover smoothly, otherwise we run the risk of loosing customers.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Feature a "Remember me" option to save login information.
      • Clear error messages with solutions.
      • "Forget password" option.
  4. Users have imperfect memories.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      People don't have the best memory, especially the generations going forward. As such, our website must account for this so they don't have a hard time accessing data across multiple pages.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Moderately relevant. New users might struggle to locate services or pricing, but returning users will have an easier time.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Breadcrumb navigation so users know where they are.
      • Recently viewed sections for portfolio pages.
      • Consistent layout.
  5. Users are social animals.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      This implies that people trust recommendations, testimonials, and social proof, and that they are likely to share their experiences with others.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Highly relevant. Potential clients need to see that others have had positive experiences with our services, and they themselves need to have a positive experience so that they can share with others.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Implement a testimonials sections.
      • Social media integration to show activity and engagement.
      • Trust badges or partner logos to establish credibility (Example: Partnered with Canva).
  6. Users have selective attention.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      Users do not have limitless attention spans, as such they scan rather than read everything. They focus only on what seems relevant to their goals and ignore clutter.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Very relevant. If users don't immediately see the value, they may leave without engaging (Example: Leaving a youtube video because it doesn't immediately answer your question or grab your attention).

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Bold and concise headings to capture attention.
      • Bullet points and icons to break up text.
      • F-shaped reading pattern optimization.
  7. Users crave information.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      Users seek knowledge before making a decision, especially for services like web development and design. This means they need to be reassured before committing to a single company especially since they have to pay.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Highly relevant. Clients need to understand our process, pricing, and expertise before committing. A miscommunication here will cause issues.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Virtual Assistant and easy access to an actual assistant.
      • Service comparison tables for clarity.
      • FAQs and tooltips to preemptively answer common questions.
  8. Users are driven by their unconscious mind.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      Emotions and subconscious cues influence decision-making more than logic alone. This means that our site should evoke positive emotions and trust to encourage users to take action.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Very relevant. Users are more likely to engage and trust a visually appealing and psychologically reassuring site.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Testimonials.
      • Warm, inviting color schemes that build trust.
      • Rewards for completing a task or first time discounts.
      • Previews and/or free downloads.
  9. Users rely on mental models.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      People create mental models to gauge how things should work. This means that our site should follow common design patterns and conventions.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Very relevant. Users expect certain elements to be in certain places, and if they aren't, they may become confused or make mistakes.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Consistent UI elements across all pages.
      • Standardized form layouts.
      • Use familiar icons and labels.
  10. Users have imperfect vision.
    1. What does it mean in the context of your site?

      Users may have visual impairments, small screens, or low attention spans, affecting how they engage with content. This means that our site should be accessible and responsive to all users.

    2. How relevant is it to your site content and audience?

      Very relevant. Accessibility and readability are crucial for a professional site.

    3. What techniques can you use to implement your response?
      • Alt text for images to assist screen readers.
      • Keyboard-friendly navigation.
      • Large, legible fonts.
      • High contrast color schemes.
