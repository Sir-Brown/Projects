Branding Plan

Company Audience and Market Positioning

Who are our audience?

Our current audience includes small business owners, freelancers, and entrepreneurs looking to establish a strong online/marketing presence, or obtain high quality code. Future customers may expand to larger businesses, programmers or tech companies, and experienced content creators who need high-quality web design, branding, and coding solutions.

What makes us unique?

What sets us apart is our personalized approach to web design, ensuring that each site is custom built, visually engaging, and optimized for user experience and accessibility. We welcome a user-centered design process and let the clients, if they choose, to be a part of the creation of their product to guarantee maximum satisfaction.

How to maintain customer loyalty?

To maintain customer loyalty, we will ensure consistent quality, timely delivery, transparent communication, and ongoing support. All going well customers will share the company via word of mouth and this will build trust as prospective clients already trust their friends and family who trust us.

How to promote our brand?

We will promote our brand through targeted digital marketing, including social media campaigns, content marketing, partnerships, and word-of-mouth referrals. Portfolio showcases, and client testimonials will further strengthen credibility and attract new customers.

Company Brand: Logo

Describe the current state of your company logo (rough, refined, weak, strong):

The current logo is moderate in strength. It could use some refinement to make it more memorable.

Where it makes most sense to place it on your site pages:

The placement of the logo varies based on the page. For example, on the home page, it could be centered while in the upper left corner for the other pages, or it could be the later for all pages as that is the familiar placement and would prevent overthinking in clients.

How the logo colors will be reflected in the site color scheme:

The colors used for the logo would be from the same color palette created for the website. As such, it fit the theme of the page It would be on the lighter side to contrast the website's darker colors or vise versa if applicable.

What you plan to include in your brand style guide:

A brand style guide establishes the visual and tonal identity of a business. As such, it would include the logo, color palette, typography, imagery, and other graphics or forms of media, as well as website design elements.
